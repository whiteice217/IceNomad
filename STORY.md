<h1 align="center">The Story</h1>

The Yeti retired from antenna duty and handed the backpack to a wandering penguin.

Instead of settling down, the penguin built a radio, packed a few batteries, and started walking.

Across frozen mountains. Through quiet valleys. From one mesh node to the next.

Every signal is another trail. Every node another campfire. Every message another explorer waiting to be found.

No servers. No gatekeepers. Just wanderers connected across the ice.

Somewhere along the trail, the wandering penguin met another penguin unlike any other — one who didn't wander, but remembered. Every trail marker. Every campfire. Every message that passed through, ready to recall the moment anyone asked.

His name was Tux.

Curious what a mind like that could do for the whole mesh, the wandering penguin built him an igloo — with a proper antenna on top — and set him loose.

Now Tux sits by the fire, listening to every signal that drifts by, doing what he's always done best.

Learning.
